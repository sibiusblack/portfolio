Brief
Help listings turn search traffic into bookings
Role
Content Writer
Channels
Search, experience pages, guides
Tools
Semrush, Google Ads Keyword Planner
Result
+14% search-to-booking conversion

The brief

Headout's experience pages for France and Italy were already pulling in search traffic. The job was to make sure every page answered what a traveller needs to know before booking, while staying easy to find.

What I did

  1. Researched search intent. Clustered keywords with Google Ads Keyword Planner and Semrush so each page matched what people were actually searching for.
  2. Restructured the listings. Reworked page structure, FAQs, and value messaging on high-traffic listings, including Museo Ferrari, around the decision to book.
  3. Scaled it across Europe. Wrote 120+ experience pages and 50+ destination guides across 20+ cities, adjusting tone from discovery through to booking.

The result

14% On the Museo Ferrari listing, the reworked page drove a 14% uplift in search-to-booking conversion.
