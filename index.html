About

I've spent the past four years writing content for travel, tech, B2B, and the arts. I started out writing stories for a virtual heritage museum, then moved into agency work at Panorbit, where I handled content strategy and campaigns for clients including Reliance. Most recently, I was at Headout writing travel experience pages and destination guides, with a focus on SEO and on helping people go from browsing to booking.

Since moving to Toronto, I've been volunteering as a writer for a local music festival, working on sponsorship and promotional content.

My work usually sits somewhere between research, SEO, and writing. I like taking something complex or unfamiliar and making it clear and enjoyable to read, and I like seeing how it performs once it's out in the world.
