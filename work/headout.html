The brief

The Museo Ferrari listing was drawing plenty of search traffic, but too few of those visitors went on to book. Looking closely, the page had four problems.

  • Missing or unclear information. Visitors couldn't easily find what they needed to decide.
  • A confusing structure. Key details were buried or out of order.
  • Weak reasons to book. The value of the experience didn't come through.
  • FAQs that missed the point. They didn't answer the questions travellers actually asked.

What I did

  1. Listened before writing. Studied search data in Semrush and Google Ads Keyword Planner, customer reviews and questions, competitor listings, and the page's performance data to see where visitors were dropping off.
  2. Restructured the page around the booking decision. Reordered the layout so the details travellers look for come first and nothing important is buried.
  3. Rewrote the value messaging. Made the reasons to book clear and specific, so the experience sold itself.
  4. Rebuilt the FAQs from real questions. Drew on reviews and customer questions to answer doubts before they stopped a booking.
  5. Matched search intent. Aligned headings and copy with how people search for the museum, so the page stayed easy to find.

I owned the page copy end to end, from research through to the final rewrite.
