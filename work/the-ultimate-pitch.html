The brief

The Ultimate Pitch is Reliance's national B-school competition, and Season X drew 22.8K+ participants. It needed one campaign that could carry from the first call for entries through to finals day.

What I did

  1. Planned it end to end. Owned the content strategy and editorial calendar for the full two-month run.
  2. Kept one message across every channel. Shaped campaign messaging for Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube, plus event videos, pitch decks, OOH, and event collateral, with short-form scripts for in-house video.
  3. Covered finals day live. Ran live social coverage of the finals so the campaign ended at its peak.
