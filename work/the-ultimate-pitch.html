The brief

The Ultimate Pitch is Reliance's national B-school competition. For Season X, the campaign had four jobs to do over two months.

  • Drive registrations Get B-school students across the country to sign up and compete.
  • Build buzz Grow awareness of the competition on and off campus.
  • Keep participants engaged Hold attention through every round, up to the finals.
  • Showcase Reliance Put the brand in front of students and campuses.

What I did

  1. Planned the campaign in phases. Built the editorial calendar around three stages: launch, competition rounds, and finals.
  2. Adapted it for each channel. LinkedIn carried the career angle, Instagram spoke to students, and YouTube held longer videos and recaps.
  3. Wrote across formats. Social posts and captions, video scripts, pitch decks, event collateral, and OOH.
  4. Covered the finals live. Ran real-time social coverage on finals day.
  5. Coordinated the team. Worked with designers, the video and event teams, and Reliance's team to keep everything on message.

The result

  • 155K+social reach across Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube in two months.
  • 22.8K+B-school students registered to compete.

What I learned: One story, many channels. The message stayed the same from launch to finals; what changed was how each channel told it, with career stakes on LinkedIn, energy on Instagram, and depth on YouTube. Planning those versions up front kept a two-month campaign coherent across every format and every team.
