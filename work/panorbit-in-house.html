Building an agency's voice from the inside

Panorbit Instagram, blog, eBooks, and case studies

Brief
Give the agency a voice of its own, on social and on its website
Role
Writer, scriptwriter, on-screen presenter, and channel manager
Formats
Reels, blogs, eBooks, case studies
Channels
Instagram, Panorbit website
Timeline
2022–2025, alongside client work

The brief

Panorbit spent its days building brands for clients. Its own brand needed the same care with content that showed prospective clients what the agency knew, and showed future teammates what it was like to work there.

  • Show the expertise Lead with real marketing knowledge, so every post teaches something.
  • Show the people Give a face to the team and the culture behind the work.
  • Earn the scroll Stand out in feeds full of other agencies.
  • Sound like one brand Keep the same voice from a 30-second reel to a full eBook.

What I did

  1. Scripted the founder's reels. Turned lessons on marketing leadership into short micro-dramas, with the founder on screen.
  2. Wrote and presented a campaign series. Broke down popular Indian marketing campaigns in short, educational reels, in front of the camera.
  3. Created a culture series. Named the series, acted in it, and helped script it, giving the team a lighter, more personal presence.
  4. Ran the channel. Published every reel on Panorbit's Instagram and monitored how each one landed.
  5. Carried the voice onto the website. Wrote blogs, eBooks, and case studies in the same playful, pop-culture voice, including a case study on a project that predated me, written from interviews with the founders and designers.

The result

Over one to two years, the content became part of how Panorbit introduced itself, to clients and to future teammates alike.

  • 50+ reels across three series Scripted, filmed, and published as a steady part of the brand's feed.
  • Used well beyond the feed Shared in client pitches and proposals, and in hiring and employer branding.
  • Built to last The series continued after I handed them over.
  • Well received Positive feedback from the founder, the team, and the audience.

What I learned: Owning a brand's content end to end changes how you write. Scripting, posting, and watching how each reel landed showed me what the audience responded to, and that feedback shaped the next piece, whether it was a 30-second reel or a full eBook. Keeping one voice across every format taught me that consistency is what turns individual posts into a recognizable brand.
